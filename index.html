Which Companies Sell the Best Replacement Windows in Vancouver in 2026?

Vancouver and Metro Vancouver service area

Our 2026 Vancouver shortlist features 10 window companies serving the city and surrounding Lower Mainland communities. Replacing windows in Vancouver is about more than choosing a frame colour. Whether you are replacing a few drafty windows or planning a whole-home renovation, compare product specifications, installation methods, warranty terms and detailed quotes before you decide.

How to choose windows for Vancouver’s climate

Vancouver homeowners should focus on dependable air and water sealing, durable exterior finishing, effective condensation management and suitable insulating glass. Ask each contractor to explain the proposed installation method, flashing details and how it addresses water around existing openings. Window performance is a combination of the selected product and the quality of the installation.

How much does window replacement cost in Vancouver in 2026?

Public 2026 guides show materially different ranges because they include different window types and installation assumptions. One Vancouver-specific guide cites approximately CAD $800 to $2,500+ per installed window, while another scenario-based estimate for eight medium casements works out to approximately CAD $686 to $928 each. Questions to ask before signing a window replacement contract

  1. Which exact window models and glazing packages are included?
  2. Will you use full-frame replacement or a retrofit/insert, and why?
  3. Who performs the installation, and is the installation team insured?
  4. How will you manage exterior flashing, waterproofing and trim?
  5. What is covered by the written product and installation warranty?
  6. Does the quote include disposal, interior finishing and applicable taxes?
  7. What is the payment schedule and estimated installation window?
