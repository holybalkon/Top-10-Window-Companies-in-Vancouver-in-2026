Common questions about replacing windows in Vancouver

Public 2026 guides show materially different ranges because they include different window types and installation assumptions. One Vancouver-specific guide cites approximately CAD $800 to $2,500+ per installed window, while another scenario-based estimate for eight medium casements works out to approximately CAD $686 to $928 each. Treat these as indicative third-party benchmarks, not quotes from any company on this site.

Price drivers include window size and operation, vinyl versus composite versus custom alternatives, glazing configuration, the condition of the opening, full-frame versus insert work, accessibility and exterior finishing, and any permit or structural requirements.

Focus on dependable air and water sealing, durable exterior finishing, effective condensation management and suitable insulating glass. Window performance is a combination of the selected product and the quality of the installation.

Full-frame replacement removes the existing window including its frame, while a retrofit or insert installation fits the new window into the existing frame. Ask each contractor which method they propose for your home and why.

Ask how exterior flashing, waterproofing and trim will be handled, and how the contractor addresses water around existing openings.

Ask for an itemized, project-specific estimate showing product, glazing, removal, full-frame or insert installation, trim and disposal. Also confirm whether it includes interior finishing and applicable taxes, plus the payment schedule and estimated installation window.
